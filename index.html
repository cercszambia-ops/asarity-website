01 / ABOUT ASARITY

Safety is more than a product. It is part of how a business operates.

A practical partner for safer workplaces.

Asarity Safety Wear Solutions Limited is a Zambian supplier of Personal Protective Equipment (PPE), industrial workwear and workplace safety solutions.

We support corporate, institutional and commercial clients by helping them source products suited to their operational requirements. Our approach is built on professionalism, reliability, integrity and customer-focused service.

We aim to build long-term partnerships through responsive communication, competitive quotations and dependable supply arrangements.
